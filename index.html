About

I own the SSE Airtricity app, used by Home Energy customers across the Republic of Ireland and Northern Ireland. I also lead Enhanced Smart Insights, an award-winning programme that turns smart meter data into analysis and AI-driven energy-saving advice.

Before product, I spent years in digital change across telecoms, banking and energy. That's where I learned to define roadmaps, prioritise what creates the most customer and business value, and bring UX, engineering and operations teams with me.
