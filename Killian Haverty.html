About

I own products that turn energy data into decisions customers can act on.

I'm the Business Product Owner for the SSE Airtricity app, used by Home Energy customers across the Republic of Ireland and Northern Ireland. I also lead Enhanced Smart Insights, an award-winning programme that uses smart meter data to give customers analysis and AI-driven energy-saving recommendations.

My background is in digital change across telecoms, banking and energy. I define roadmaps, prioritise the features that create the most customer and business value, and work closely with UX, engineering and operations teams to ship them.
